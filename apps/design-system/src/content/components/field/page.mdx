## Usage

Use [Text Field](/components/text-field) for a standard labeled text input. Use `Field` when a
field needs compound controls or a custom structure. Compose `Field.Root` with `Field.Label`,
`Field.Description`, and `Field.Error`. Connect non-native controls through their accessible label
and description props.

Use [Fieldset](/components/fieldset) to group related fields. `Field` describes one field or compound
control; `Fieldset` describes a group of related fields.
